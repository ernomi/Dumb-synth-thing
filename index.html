Mapping
  • ShapeBlends four waves by degree: round toward sine, box-like toward square, triangle-like toward triangle, spiky toward saw.
  • Object colorWarm hues (red, orange, yellow) add overdrive. Cool hues (blue, teal) stay clean.
  • Scene colorA warm image adds 2nd harmonics and fuller low mids. A cool image adds 3rd harmonics, more treble and scooped mids.
  • SizeBigger objects play lower notes, smaller ones higher.
  • DistanceFarther objects get more reverb, less treble and less volume.

Distance is estimated from how high an object sits in the frame (lower means closer, as on a tabletop). Objects are found by contrast or by a closed outline, so even a white switch on a white wall can be picked up.
